The finding said RCE. The second tester asked one question and the ticket died.
<!-- SC_OFF --><div class="md"><p>Last year I inherited a “critical RCE” from an automated pass.</p> <p>The request looked perfect. Parameter <strong>host</strong>. Payload <strong>8.8.8.8; sleep 5</strong>. Status <strong>200</strong>. Response time jumped from ~800ms to ~6s. The scanner wrote OS command injection, confidence high. The client had already seen the word critical in the draft.</p> <p>I replayed it once. Same delay. Replay again. 1.1s. Again. 7s. Again. 900ms. The sleep was not in the response body. <strong>id</strong> came back as the literal string <strong>id</strong>. <strong>expr 41 + 1</strong> came back as the literal string. The page was a diagnostics form. It echoed the query and, under load, the WAF in front of it spent extra time on anything that looked like a shell metacharacter.</p> <p>That is a known failure mode, not a rare one.
