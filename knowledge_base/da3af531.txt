New Attack Against RSA
<p>ArsTechnica is <a href="https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/theres-a-new-way-to-break-rsa-thats-faster-than-anything-weve-seen-before/">reporting</a> on a &#8220;new&#8221; attack against RSA, one that bypasses factoring.</p>
<p>First, this attack isn&#8217;t new. The original research is from <a href="https://eprint.iacr.org/2007/424">2007</a>. What is new is the implementation.</p>
<p>Second, it is a forgery attack. It allows an attacker to forge digital signatures. It does not recover the private key from the public key.</p>
<p>Third, the attack only works against pure signatures. That is, signatures without any formatting or padding. This is not generally how we use RSA in practice.</p>
