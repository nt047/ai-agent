Using Device Linking to Eavesdrop on WhatsApp and Signal

<blockquote><p>Apps such as WhatsApp Web and Signal Desktop allow people to use their accounts on other devices, such as laptops or desktop computers.</p>
<p>Germany&#8217;s Customs Office has been using these features to connect a police-controlled computer to a suspect&#8217;s account.</p>
<p>Once connected, messages can be delivered to that computer without the police having to crack the encryption protecting them.</p>
