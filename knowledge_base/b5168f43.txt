In a recent a16z survey of CISOs at organizations with more than 1,000 employees, 36% said they are comfortable today letting AI agents act autonomously on low-risk security actions. In 12 months from now, 60% expect to be comfortable at that level. Overall, AI SOC was the highest ranking initiative for 2027. The pressure behind the shift is easy to see. In the same survey, the most common worry was AI-accelerated attacks outpacing the team's ability to respond, selected by 55% of respondents. Analyst time is the scarcest resource in the SOC, and every alert still means pulling evidence from several systems, reconstructing what happened and deciding whether it matters.
