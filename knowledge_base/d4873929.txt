The attacker used credentials for an AWS account that Qubole's platform uses to reach those customers. How the attacker got those credentials has not been disclosed. Qubole , a data lake platform owned by Idera, operates within its customers' AWS accounts. To let it in, a customer creates an IAM role, which is an AWS identity with its own set of permissions. The role's trust policy allows Qubole's AWS account to assume the role, granting those permissions.
