The attacker needed only an ordinary Telegram account. Telegram fixed the flaw in version 12.10.4 of the app, released in late September, and NebuSec disclosed it on October 29 . The flaw can be triggered in every version from 8.6.2 through 12.10.3, NebuSec's co-founder and chief executive said in an email to The Hacker News. Those versions came out between March 2022 and September 2026. The company confirmed it in private phone calls on ARM64-based phones and has no evidence so far that it directly affects Telegram's iOS and desktop apps or group calls.
