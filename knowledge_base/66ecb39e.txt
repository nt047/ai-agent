ribe Home Blog Usable Security at Luxury Hotels Interesting case study from luxury hotels. Attackers used a fake booking request to gain access to a reservations inbox, where they copied genuine refund requests and got fraudulent ones approved. This is a good illustration why “just don’t click on links” is bad advice. It can’t work when answering strangers’ emails is the job. Even worse, in this case the guest-first culture punishes hesitation. More interesting, the case study shows that splitting security between owner, operator, and brand leaves nobody accountable. And this is exactly the setting where blaming staff becomes the convenient answer.
