FreeIPA is the system that determines who may log in across a Linux domain and maintains all identities in a 389 Directory Server database accessed via LDAP. The attack needs a second flaw in that database software. The FreeIPA project has already fixed its side in version 4.13.4 . Red Hat says it reproduced the chain twice on a default installation, most recently on a machine with no access at all.
