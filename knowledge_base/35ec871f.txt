And more and more of it is centralized: the same systems affecting many different aspects of our lives and society. In many cases, it just barely works. And when it fails, it tends to fail badly. There are a lot of technological reasons why this is true, but they are all downstream of the economic ones. The technologies we rely on are built and maintained within our economic system—and that’s where the core problem lies. Security, safety, resilience, redundancy, recoverability—these are all essential parts of reliable technology, but they are all costs. More importantly, they are inefficient: costs that feel wasteful until something bad happens. Modern shareholder capitalism, and private equity in particular, squeeze all inefficiencies out of a company in pursuit of near-term profits.
