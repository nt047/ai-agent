And more and more of it is centralized, meaning that individual failures have cascading consequences across many different aspects of our lives and society. A lot of our tech infrastructure just barely works. And when it fails, it can fail badly. It’s the very opposite of resilience. There are a lot of technological reasons why this is true, but they are all downstream from the economic ones. The technologies we rely on are built and maintained within our economic system—and that’s where the core problems lie.
