They then used insecure Active Directory Certificate Services (AD CS) settings to obtain login certificates for privileged accounts, including one for a domain controller. Microsoft said in a report that it investigated two such intrusions, one of which resulted in company data being copied to an external server. The attackers exploited no flaw in Teams itself. They relied on social engineering and on AD CS that had been set up insecurely, and Microsoft did not tie the activity to any known group.
