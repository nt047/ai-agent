The person being called does not have to answer or touch their phone for it to work, but the caller must already be one of their WeChat contacts. Calif reported the flaw to Tencent in July and says the company has since blocked the exploit for all users. No attacks using the flaw have been reported, and Calif does not say there were any. Attacks that require no action from the target, known as zero-click attacks, are not new. Last year, WhatsApp patched a flaw it said may have been used in targeted attacks.
