The stealer is built to take the secrets and cloud credentials that a repository's CI workflows can reach. It ran in at least 1,806 repositories, where the attacker has likely collected those credentials, the company says. Each attack started with a pull request titled docs: dependency audit notes, opened from a branch named wfx-docs to test whether the account could write to the repository.
