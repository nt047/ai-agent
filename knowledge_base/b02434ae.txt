 Us On Social Media       RSS Feeds  Email Alerts Retadup Worm Resurfaces 7 Years After Takedown to Spread CoffeeLoader Malware  Ravie Lakshmanan  Nov 16, 2026 Threat Intelligence / Endpoint Security Cybersecurity researchers have disclosed details of a new variant of CoffeeLoader , a malware designed to deliver additional payloads to infected machines. "On launch, the NSIS-based installer executable dropped a file named '7z.dll' into a temporary directory and ran it through rundll32.exe," Kaspersky said in an analysis published today. "The '7z.dll' file was a patched version of the original library of the popular 7-Zip archiver. Part of its code had been replaced with malicious self-decrypting shellcode that turned out to be a new version of the Armoury packer." CoffeeLoader was first documented by Zscaler ThreatLabz in March 2025, detailing its use of the Armoury packer to resist analysis and ultimately run Rhadamanthys shellcode.
