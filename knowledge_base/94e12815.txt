The contest pays researchers to show working exploits and passes the flaws to the vendors. One of the three Pixel exploits earned Ikotas Labs $300,000, the contest's top prize, and made the team the overall winner. Trend Micro's Zero Day Initiative (ZDI), which runs Pwn2Own, posted the results but had not published how the three exploits work as of October 9. The wins were demonstrations on contest phones, and at least two of the three used a bug that was already known before the attempt.
