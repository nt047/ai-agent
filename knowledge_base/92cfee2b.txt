Follow Us On Social Media       RSS Feeds  Email Alerts Synology NAS Devices Targeted in Ransomware Attacks Exploiting Patched DSM Flaw  Ravie Lakshmanan  Nov 03, 2026 Ransomware / Cyber Attack Synology is urging customers to update the DiskStation Manager (DSM) software powering its Network Attached Storage (NAS) devices following ransomware attacks. The Taiwanese vendor said it observed threat actors exploiting a vulnerability patched in December 2024 to target devices running outdated versions of DSM. "These attacks aim to encrypt data and extort ransom," it said in a statement released last week. While Synology did not disclose the exact security flaw that's being exploited in these attacks, the company linked to a November 2024 security advisory that listed three vulnerabilities - CVE-2024-10441 (CVSS score: 9.8) - An improper encoding or escaping of output vulnerability in the system plugin daemon of DSM that could lead to remote code execution via unspecified vectors
