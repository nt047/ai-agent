Tracked as CVE-2026-119618 , it affects versions 2.4.55 through 2.4.70 and is fixed in version 2.4.71, released yesterday. Only servers with HTTP/2 enabled are affected, according to Red Hat, which ships the server as part of Red Hat Enterprise Linux. An attacker who can reach the server needs no authentication to exploit the flaw and could take full control of the server process, the Linux vendor says. It rates the flaw 9.8 out of 10 on the CVSS scale.
