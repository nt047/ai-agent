It’s not because your team and tools aren’t sophisticated enough. It’s the reason we have detections in the first place. And, it’s why once we accept that prevention has its limits, we arrive at an interdiction strategy: finding and disrupting what got in. After the alert The alert tells you something happened. However, gaining access to your network doesn’t necessarily mean attackers have stolen data or disrupted the systems they were targeting. They may need to obtain additional access, move to another machine, or further explore the network. Being able to see and understand that activity is the intervention opportunity.
