Follow Us On Social Media       RSS Feeds  Email Alerts Binance Warns of iOS Sandbox Escape That Could Expose Crypto Wallet Keys, No Fix Confirmed  Swati Khandelwal  Nov 10, 2026 Vulnerability / Mobile Security An iOS sandbox escape published on GitHub lets an app read other apps' files on iPhones running iOS 26 and 27. That puts seed phrases, private keys, and login credentials at risk of exposure to malicious apps, Binance warned today. Apple's updates for iOS 26 and 27, released yesterday, do not list a fix. A researcher who goes by forcequit , in collaboration with 0xjohnny , released the escape, called worse_query , on Sunday, a day before those updates came out. Neither the exchange nor the researcher reported any attacks or thefts involving it. The exchange told users to delete apps installed from untrusted sources, such as sideloaded or manually signed apps, and to update iOS and all their apps. No fix for the escape has been confirmed.
