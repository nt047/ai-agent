Quay enables anonymous access by default, and while it is enabled, the attacker needs no account. When it is off, anyone with a valid account can still exploit the flaw, Red Hat said yesterday. The company has released fixed builds for eight Quay 3 branches. The flaw is tracked as CVE-2026-123710 (CVSS score: 9.8). Quay uses keys it trusts to sign the access tokens that allow image pushes and pulls. The flaw lets an attacker add their own key to the keys Quay trusts, with no expiry date.
