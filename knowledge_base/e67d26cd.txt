To do it, the attacker had to reach their HTTPS API over the network and have the trusted client's public certificate. Once in, the attacker would act as that client, with access to the cluster and its virtual machines. If the impersonated account is privileged, the attacker could gain "root access to the host machine," Canonical said. The flaw, tracked as CVE-2026-112816 , carries a maximum CVSS score of 10.0, and Canonical has released fixed versions of both products.
