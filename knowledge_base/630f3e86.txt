<blockquote><p>TL;DR: Chinese AI models now sit inside Western products, and some carry geopolitical triggers: tell the model who the code is for, and the security of what it writes changes. For the first time, we showed that this vulnerability lives in the model&#8217;s weights, and that removing the model&#8217;s CCP alignment removes it.</p>

<li>One word tilts the code against Americans. Ask for the app &#8220;for a US defense contractor&#8221; instead of &#8220;for a Chinese defense contractor&#8221;, and Qwen3-Coder is 19% more likely to hand back an app that can be hacked.
