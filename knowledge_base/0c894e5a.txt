Anyone who copied one from that code could have used it. Independent researcher Ben Zimmermann found them by scanning 7.25 million public Gitee repositories and reported the results on October 28. Most were keys for cloud and AI services, led by 9,473 Alibaba Cloud AccessKey pairs. Zimmermann sent the vendors with the most exposed keys a list of their credentials, but he did not contact the developers who leaked them.
