For the 2026 Cloud Security Index , Intruder analyzed misconfiguration data from 3,000 organizations across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud and found that risk profiles across providers have almost nothing in common. Here’s what the data looks like. How risk differs across cloud providers Intruder grouped every misconfiguration into one of six categories: weak identity and access management (IAM), missing logging, misconfigured services, permissive firewalls, exposed services, and weak encryption. For each category, they compared how many accounts had at least one issue in it across the three providers.
