Use Strong Passwords
<!-- SC_OFF --><div class="md"><p>A quick reminder I keep seeing people overlook: your password manager is only as strong as the one password you actually remember.</p> <p>If you use the same master password across your password manager, your email, and a couple of old forums, a single credential leak from 2019 can unravel the whole setup. The vault encryption is solid. The recovery flow usually isn’t.</p> <p>What I do instead:</p> <ul> <li>Master password exists nowhere else. Not in the vault. Not written down digitally. Not a variation of an old one.</li> <li>2FA on the email account that can reset the vault. If someone owns your email, they own the reset chain.</li> <li>Recovery codes stored offline. Paper or a hardware-encrypted drive. Not a screenshot in the camera roll.</li> </ul> <p>The uncomfortable part is that most breaches aren‘t breaking AES.
