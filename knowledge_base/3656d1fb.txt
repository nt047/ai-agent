An event bus that never drops the critical stuff: QoS and backpressure in pwnproxy
<!-- SC_OFF --><div class="md"><p>An intercepting proxy generates events nonstop: every request produces a <em>flow</em>, every flow can produce <em>findings</em>, and every finding triggers storage, triage and real-time notifications. If the bus connecting all of it blocks when one consumer is slow, the whole proxy stalls. If it drops messages carelessly, you lose security findings.
