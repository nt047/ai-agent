The Garante, Italy's data protection authority, found that the feature was enabled by default and that the app accessed Italian users' photos, videos, and related metadata without their explicit consent. The company has 60 days to switch the feature off by default, including for people who have already granted the app gallery access, and to request consent separately. Meta plans to appeal and calls the fine "disproportionate", a spokesperson said in a statement reported by Reuters.
