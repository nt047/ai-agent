The kernel's own CVE team rates it 9.8 out of 10 on the CVSS scale. Anthropic said on October 27 that its AI model wrote an exploit that uses the flaw to let a remote attacker with no credentials run commands as root on Linux servers with a multipath TCP listener. As of October 29, Ubuntu and RHEL have not released a fixed kernel. Red Hat, Amazon, Oracle, and SUSE score the flaw between 5.9 and 8.1, lower than the kernel team's 9.8.
