France had more Internet-exposed Danfoss refrigeration dashboards than the rest of the world combined before the supermarket attacks (weekly IPv4 scan data)

France had 1,331 (53%).</li> <li>85% of the French ones ran software with known flaws, including 527 end-of-life AK-SM 800 units. Only 195 had every fix. Elsewhere it was 84%, so France had more of them online, not worse patching.</li> <li>1,019 of the French addresses were already open on March 30.</li> <li>October 14 scan: 316 still online in France (−76%, against a normal 3–7% weekly loss).
