The bug, tracked as CVE-2026-134624 , is a server-side request forgery (SSRF) flaw. It allowed a user with high-level API access to open connections to internal systems of the user's choosing on the platform's servers. The company fixed the flaw and restored access to the dashboard and APIs late on Saturday, closing an outage that began last Monday. Email delivery and spam filtering kept working throughout, according to the company. It has not said whether the flaw was the attackers' way in.
