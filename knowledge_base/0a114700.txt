Follow Us On Social Media       RSS Feeds  Email Alerts Trezor Confirms Hidden Implants in CryptoBilis-Sold Wallets That Can Steal Recovery Phrases  Swati Khandelwal  Nov 13, 2026 Cryptocurrency / Supply Chain Three Trezor Safe 5 wallets bought from the reseller CryptoBilis contain an implant, an extra circuit board that can copy their recovery phrases, hardware wallet maker Trezor said yesterday. The implant reads the phrase from the wallet's screen and sends it over the mobile network to a server controlled by the attackers. The company examined one of the wallets in its lab, identified the other two from photos their owners sent, and has had no reports of lost funds. A firmware update released earlier this week, version 2.13.1, stops the implants found so far from capturing anything on wallets that run it, the company said, but “the update can’t protect a wallet backup that was captured before it.” The company has not changed its advice to anyone
