AI Security
<p>Two weeks ago, I attended the <a href="https://unpromptedcon.org/">[un]prompted</a> AI security conference in San Francisco. It was an excellent event: three days of very dense talks about all aspects of AI security. (My talk was about integrous AI, and I will post it when it is online.)</p>
<p>AI security can be divided into three broad topics. It&#8217;s important for researchers to understand which one they are working on. And it&#8217;s important for us as a community to work on them all. They are: security of AI, security with AI, and security from AI. [un]prompted had good talks about all three...</p>
