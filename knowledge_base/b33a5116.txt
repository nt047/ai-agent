Petersburg, Russia. Operating since September 2022, the Russia-based ransomware group Qilin is the currently the most industrious ransomware-as-a-service gang, according to a report (PDF) this month by the cybersecurity firm NCC Group (KrebsOnSecurity profiled the administrator of the second most active ransomware group — The Gentlemen — in June). The Qilin ransomware administrator “Haise” advertising his affiliate program on the cybercrime forum RAMP. Image: Ke-la.com. Qilin attracts affiliates to hack into companies and deploy ransomware by offering them at least 80 percent of any extortion amount paid by victims. Its members practice the “double extortion” model, stealing sensitive data before encrypting systems and threatening to publish the files online. Unlike most ransomware partnerships based out of Russia, Qilin is happy to team with English-speaking affiliates, and its leader has shared on Telegram that they’ve worked with cybercrime groups like Scattered Spider and RansomHub
