Pinpointing Location of Videos from Background Airplane Sounds
<p>This is <a href="https://gizmodo.com/a-youtuber-figured-out-how-to-find-almost-anyone-using-airplane-sounds-2000835861">fascinating</a>:</p>
<blockquote><p>The video describes a technique that can reveal where an otherwise unidentifiable recording was made by analyzing its audio for the faint sounds of passing aircraft. The technique is both fascinating and frightening.</p>
<p>It works by looking at Doppler curves, which plot the way a plane’s sound changes as it passes overhead because of the Doppler effect. This is the same familiar Doppler effect that causes the frequency of a siren to rise as it approaches and then fall as it passes by and moves away. More generally, it’s the phenomenon whereby the frequency of a wave perceived by an observer changes when the observer and the source of the wave are moving in relation to one another...</p></blockquote>
