Each flaw needs a specific setup on a virtual server: an iRule that uses a particular command, or an APM policy that includes two particular kinds of agent. F5, which makes BIG-IP , disclosed the flaws today and says they may also allow remote code execution. The company has released engineering hotfixes and lists no workaround. The company rates all six 9.8 under CVSS v3.1 and 9.3 under CVSS v4.0. They are stack-based buffer overflows in BIG-IP's data plane, the part that processes almost all of its network traffic.
