The first security updates with the fix came out on August 17. Neither Apple nor the two researchers who found the flaw report that it was used in attacks. Nik Tsytsarkin of Meta Red Team X published the technical details last week. Mustafa Calap , a security engineer who reported the same flaw separately, published his own analysis the same day. On iPhones, an orange indicator shows when an app is using the microphone. On one of the researchers' earlier test builds, that indicator stayed off while the app recorded.
