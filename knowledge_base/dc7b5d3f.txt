Follow Us On Social Media       RSS Feeds  Email Alerts Signal Blocks AI Agents From Reading Private Chats via Screenshots on Android and iOS  Ravie Lakshmanan  Nov 21, 2026 Privacy / Mobile Security Signal has announced that it will restrict screenshots by default to stop artificial intelligence (AI) agents from reading private chats on Android and iOS devices, but conceded that the protection is "incomplete." The feature applies to agentic systems built into iOS 27 and on Android versions where the encrypted messaging platform can detect an AI assistant attempting to read the Signal screen. It also builds upon a similar setting that Signal introduced in Windows 11 last May following Microsoft's release of the controversial Recall feature. Calling the agentic integrations into Android, iOS, and Windows an "existential threat to Signal," Meredith Whittaker, president of the Signal Foundation, said the move is designed to uphold its privacy guarantees in the AI era.
