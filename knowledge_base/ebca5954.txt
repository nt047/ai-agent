Nearly 30–40% of the cases investigated by the cybersecurity company involve the use of *.onmicrosoft[.]com , a permanent fallback domain automatically generated by Microsoft when a Microsoft 365 tenant is created. These domains masquerade as IT infrastructure, opting for a help desk- or support-themed word as the tenant name to increase their credibility - helpdeskteam365.onmicrosoft[.]com itendpoint365.onmicrosoft[.]com itcoreportal.on
