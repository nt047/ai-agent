How Technology Empowers—and Imperils—Dictators
<p><em>This essay was written with Seva Gunitsky, and originally appeared in <a href="https://www.foreignaffairs.com/united-states/artificial-intelligence-autocrat-technology-empowers-dictators">Foreign Affairs</a>.</em></p>

She held up a hand-drawn sign behind the anchor&#8217;s head that read: &#8220;Stop the war. Don&#8217;t believe propaganda. They&#8217;re lying to you!&#8221; She shouted, &#8220;No to war!&#8221; until she was dragged away.</p>
