Ghostscript is the interpreter that many Linux servers use to convert uploaded PDFs, PostScript files, and images into other formats, often without the application calling it directly. Security firm pwn.ai showed this week that ordinary upload, preview, and print features in about ten common applications could be tricked into feeding it such a file. The flaw, tracked as CVE-2026-125396 , was fixed on September 8, and there have been no reports of it being used in attacks.
