Rockstar Games has now been compromised several different ways since 2018, and none of them were a zero-day
<!-- SC_OFF --><div class="md"><p>Four incidents, four completely different initial access paths:</p> <ul> <li>2022, Lapsus$: MFA fatigue against an employee, then hardcoded creds and API keys sitting in plaintext in Slack and Confluence.</li> <li>Early 2023, GTA Online: P2P netcode on PC reverse-engineered into RCE via malicious packets. The fix was kernel-level BattlEye.</li> <li>April 2026, ShinyHunters: no human identity involved. Long-lived OAuth tokens stolen from a third-party SaaS vendor and replayed straight into Rockstar's Snowflake. Bearer tokens confer authority by possession alone.</li> <li>August 2026, Cyberleek: exfiltration of a playable GTA VI dev build, 13+ gameplay videos and full map data.
