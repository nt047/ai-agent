uCreator: virtualization-based Android spyware platform targeting WeChat, Telegram and other messaging apps
<!-- SC_OFF --><div class="md"><p><strong>uCreater</strong> (the vendor's own name for the product is <em>YingGai</em>) is a commercial Android surveillance platform built around <em>群控</em> (“mass control”). Branded through packages in the <code>com.ucreator.*</code> namespace, it is not a single malware family but a productized framework: a VirtualApp/LSPatch engine, a library of per-application hook modules, a Redis and HTTP control plane, a cloud-phone farm for live screen control, and a Windows operator console. Customers can rent the platform and deploy its capabilities against different applications on a target device. Despite this breadth and multi year usage, I found little public threat reporting documenting the platform's architecture.</p> </div><!-- SC_ON --> &#32; submitted by &#32; <a href="https://www.reddit.com/user/0x0v1"> /u/0x0v1 </a> <br /> <span><a href="http
