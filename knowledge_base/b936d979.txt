Attackers Used Optimizely to A/B Test Malware on Top Websites
At least 178 top web destinations unwittingly served a variety of malware exploits against visitors over the past 24 hours thanks to a security vulnerability involving Optimizely, a company that helps websites customize user experiences and test out new features. Experts say the incident, which saw data-stealing malicious software silently injected into sites like the BBC, CNN, Equifax, Ledger and Zoom, was exacerbated by the reality that few popular websites enforce strict rules on the sources of third-party content.
