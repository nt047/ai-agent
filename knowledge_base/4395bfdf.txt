Teaching network intrusion in the funnest way possible
<!-- SC_OFF --><div class="md"><p>I wanted to share a project I’ve been working on that I’m super excited about:</p> <p>Project RedTeam: Contract Offensive</p> <p>There’s a free Demo that provides a tutorial and lets you play a few contracts (no time limit, play as much as you want). Some players are already pulling some serious hours in the demo!</p> <p>At its core, this is a game about using MITRE ATT&amp;CK adversarial techniques against procedurally generated networks. It's delivered in a gameplay loop that plays a lot like Balatro and other card based Roguelike games. In Project RedTeam, you need to earn money to pay off debts after every contract within a run. Earn money by completing objectives, side bounties, or executing exfiltration/ransom against targets- the choice on how to be profitable is always yours.</p> <p>It's a challenging but fun and fast paced take on network-intrusion cybersecurity concepts.
