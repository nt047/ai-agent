Follow Us On Social Media       RSS Feeds  Email Alerts Google Cloud Warns Three AMD Turin CPU Flaws Could Leak Data Across Processes in VMs  Swati Khandelwal  Nov 12, 2026 Vulnerability / Cloud Security Google Cloud VMs on AMD Turin chips need a guest OS patch and a stop-and-start to protect against three CPU side-channel flaws, Google said this week. The company has already applied mitigations against attacks between VMs, and between VMs and the host. Inside a VM, the flaws may let an attacker with code on the same processor core infer data from other processes or the kernel. The affected machine series are C4D, N4D, H4D, Z4D,, and G4, including bare-metal instances of C4D and Z4D. Turin is the code name for fifth-generation AMD EPYC processors. All three flaws are Transient Scheduler Attacks (TSA). Two of them, TSA-SQ (CVE-2024-36350) and TSA-L1 (CVE-2024-36357), were first disclosed on July 8, 2025, for earlier EPYC processors.
