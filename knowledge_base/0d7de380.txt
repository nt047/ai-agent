Friday Squid Blogging: I Caught a Squid
<p>On Wednesday I spent a day fishing, on a small boat out of Gloucester, MA. We caught many cod (none of which we could keep), and a bunch of hake and mackerel (all of which we could keep). And&#8230;I caught a squid! Near as I can tell, it&#8217;s a longfin squid, sometimes called a Boston squid (<i>Doryteuthis (Amerigo) pealeii</i>).</p>
<p>That night I cooked it over a barbecue grill&#8212;hot and fast. Delicious.</p>
