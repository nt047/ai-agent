We Get the Infrastructure We Pay For
<p><em>This essay originally appeared as part of a <a href="https://www.thefp.com/p/cybersecurity-terrorism-iran-china-russia-apocalypse">round table</a> in <cite>The Free Press</cite> entitled &#8220;Is America’s Infrastructure Under Attack?&#8221;</em></p>
<p>Microsoft, the FAA, banks, United Airlines, the NSA&#8212;it&#8217;s been a really bad week in cyberspace. But, amazing as it might seem, these are not related. As far as we know, they&#8217;re not the result of cyberattacks. They&#8217;re not even related to AI. (Well, that&#8217;s mostly true. The NSA will probably never tell us what went wrong.) It&#8217;s really just a series of unfortunate coincidences.</p>
