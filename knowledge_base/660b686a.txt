The intrusion, according to eSentire, begins with a redirection to a bogus site for a sniping tool ("snipp.codes-team[.]com"), from where a Microsoft-signed installer called "Snipping-Tool.exe" is downloaded. Executing the binary leads to the installation of Setup Assistant , an Electron-based application published on the Microsoft Store by ETPS Ltd. "The application was heavily obfuscated, maintained communication with C2 [command-and-control] infrastructure, monitored the victim's Downloads folder, and supported execution of C2-supplied PowerShell and JavaScript," eSentire said .
