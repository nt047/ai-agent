The attacker exploited the flaw to obtain high-level internal credentials and then, on September 24, used them to send fraudulent withdrawal commands to Bitget's wallet system. Exchanges keep most customer funds in offline cold wallets and use hot and warm wallets to process withdrawals. Transfers from those wallets must still be approved before they are signed. The stolen funds came from part of Bitget's hot and warm wallets, and its cold wallets were not affected.
