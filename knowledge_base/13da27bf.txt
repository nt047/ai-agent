Progress then depends on information the finding may lack: who owns the system, whether attackers can reach it, what protection exists, and how a change could affect the business. Each missing detail creates another investigation, another handoff, and another delay. An exposure management platform should connect discovery with a clear priority, an appropriate response, and evidence that protection has improved.
